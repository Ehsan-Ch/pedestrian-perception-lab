03 / MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED

What changes
when the light drops?

Every image is evaluated in its original form and with pixel brightness reduced to 35%. The detector and thresholds stay fixed.

This is a synthetic stress test, not a night-driving benchmark. HOG + SVM provides a classical baseline on the original scenes.

Method / conditionPrecisionRecallMissed
Small pedestrians: —

Boxes under 40 pixels tall are reported separately. The sample deliberately contains 32 images with pedestrians and 32 without; its precision does not represent natural road prevalence.

PROTOCOL

64 seeded KITTI training images · no fine-tuning · confidence 0.35 · matching IoU 0.50 · one-to-one matching · all Pedestrian annotations scored · Cyclist, Person_sitting and DontCare regions handled separately. These are fixed-threshold sample metrics, not official KITTI AP or an autonomous-driving safety validation.
